### Request and console capture

- Use `tab_checkpoint` on an Ego tab, then query captured requests and console logs with `get_requests` and `get_logs`. Capture uses Ego's native connection.
- Take a new checkpoint after the agent regains control. The previous cursor expires, and logs from the handoff period are excluded.
