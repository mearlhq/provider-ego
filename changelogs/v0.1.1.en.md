### Unified control command

- Removed the separate `mearl-ego` executable. Use `mearl browser_control --browser <browserId>` with `handoff`, `resume` or `finish` for Space lifecycle operations.
- `finish` accepts `keepTabIds` to retain result pages belonging to the task. Invalid retained pages fail explicitly; failed completion preserves the task connection.
- Control commands use a short-lived independent channel so pending page actions cannot block them. Space discovery remains responsive while page actions stay serialized.

Requires Mearl 3.0.7+, Node.js 22.23.4+ and `ego-browser` on PATH.
